How to play

  1. Take the assessment and copy the Mission Briefing it generates.
  2. Open Claude (Claude Code or Cowork) and paste the briefing as your first message. Claude becomes your Guide.
  3. Open the Guidebook and work through the levels. Claude poses each challenge — you do the building.
  4. Earn XP, level up your company, beat the boss, and demo your live business.
